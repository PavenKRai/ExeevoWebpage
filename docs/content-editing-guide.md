# Publishing content on exeevo.com (for managers)

You do not need any technical skills. Everything is done in one website: **https://exeevo.sanity.studio**
Sign in with the same account that was invited to the Sanity project.

## Add a blog post
1. Open **Blog posts** in the left menu and click the **+** (Create) button at the top.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title**. The web address is created for you. It cannot be changed after you publish.
   - **Publication date** (today by default).
   - **Summary**: one or two sentences (up to 220 characters). It appears on the cards and in Google.
   - **Topics**: pick one or more, or add a new topic under **Topics** first.
   - **Featured image** and its **Description of the image** (required: it is read aloud to people who cannot see the image).
   - **Body**: type your article. Use *Heading* and *Subheading* for sections, the list buttons for bullets, and the link button to add links. Insert images, a YouTube or Vimeo video, or a table with the **+** inside the body.
3. (Optional) open **Search and sharing** to set a different Google title and description.
4. Click **Publish** (bottom right). The page is live on the website within about a minute.

## Other content types
The same steps apply to **White papers** (upload the PDF under *PDF to download*), **Webinars** (paste the YouTube or Vimeo link under *Recording address*), **Events** (start, end, location, registration link), **Case studies**, **Press releases**, **Analyst reports** and **Glossary** terms.

## Change or remove something
- **Edit**: open the item, change it, click **Publish** again.
- **Take it offline**: open the item, click the **...** menu next to Publish and choose **Unpublish**. The page disappears from the site (visitors see "page not found"). Do not delete items that other sites might link to; unpublish them instead.
- **Undo a mistake**: open the **...** menu and choose **Review changes** or **History** (on the free plan, history only goes back about 3 days).

## Company pages
**Company pages** holds About, Partners, Careers, Privacy and Cookies. You can edit them but not add or delete them. Privacy and Cookies contain legal text: have legal review any change.

## Things to check before you publish
- Every image has a description.
- The summary is under 220 characters and says what the reader will learn.
- Statistics and claims have a source, and have been approved for use.
- You previewed the result on the site after publishing (open the page and read it through once).

## What you cannot change here
Page designs, menus, the home page and the Platform, Industries, Solutions by role and Why Exeevo pages are part of the website's code. Ask the developer for those.
